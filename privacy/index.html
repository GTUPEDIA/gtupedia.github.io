Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026. This policy explains how GTUPEDIA handles information when you use our website.

Who we are

GTUPEDIA is an independent, student-run website that organises Gujarat Technological University (GTU) exam papers and study resources. We are not affiliated with or operated by GTU. Contact: dhruvsaidava@gmail.com.

What we collect

GTUPEDIA does not require an account. We do not ask for your name, college ID, or payment details to browse papers.

When you visit the site, standard web logs and third-party services may automatically receive technical information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, and approximate location. We use this only to understand traffic and keep the site running.

Cookies & analytics

We use Google Analytics to measure how visitors use GTUPEDIA (for example, which courses are browsed most). Google Analytics may set cookies or similar identifiers on your device. You can learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy and opt out via the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Advertising

GTUPEDIA may display banner advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. We do not use pop-up or interstitial ads on GTUPEDIA.

You can manage personalised advertising at Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to GTUPEDIA and/or other sites on the Internet.

External links

Question papers and syllabi link to official GTU and other external sites. Those sites have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their practices.

Children

GTUPEDIA is intended for university and diploma students. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.

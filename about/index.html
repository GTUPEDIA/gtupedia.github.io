About GTUPEDIA

An open resource for Gujarat Technological University students — exam papers, study material, and community updates in one place, built and maintained by students and contributors across Gujarat.

Disclaimer

GTUPEDIA is not the official website of Gujarat Technological University (GTU). We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the university or any of its departments.

GTUPEDIA is an independent, student- and volunteer-run initiative created to help GTU students with practical learning, exam preparation, and exploration beyond the classroom. Content is curated and organised for convenience; question papers and official documents link to GTU sources where available.

For authoritative announcements, results, timetables, and policies, always refer to gtu.ac.in and your college.

What we do

Before exam season, the same questions come up everywhere: Where are last year’s papers? Which code is this subject? Does anyone have the syllabus? GTUPEDIA exists to answer those faster.

  • Organise official GTU question papers by course, branch, semester, and subject
  • Let you search across programmes — BE, BBA, BCA, MBA, MCA, and more as coverage grows
  • Curate learning resources, events, internships, and tools useful beyond a single textbook

Everything is free to use. No sign-up, no paywall.

Who keeps it running

GTUPEDIA is not a coaching centre or a company portal. Students from affiliated GTU colleges maintain the catalogue, add papers each exam season, fix gaps in branch data, and pass along what their campus needs most.

We’re in Beta — coverage improves every season as more courses, branches, and paper sets are added.

Where papers come from

Question papers link to official GTU sources where available. We catalogue which subjects have papers for each exam season so you know what’s ready before you click through.

If a subject or season is missing, tell us — Team GTUPEDIA and Campus Ambassadors at your college are the fastest way to get it on the list.

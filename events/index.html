Prawaas 5.0

9–11 July 2026 · Helipad Exhibition Centre (HEC), Gandhinagar, Gujarat

Towards Safe, Smart & Sustainable Public Transport

Prawaas 5.0 brings stakeholders together to expand services, modernise infrastructure, and enable inclusive access through a multimodal approach integrating buses, metro, taxis, and last-mile solutions. The event aligns government, operators, startups, technology providers, financiers, and civil society to build a mobility ecosystem that is efficient, inclusive, climate-conscious, and future-ready. India’s flagship multimodal transport show — fifth edition at Gandhinagar.

Organized by Bus and Car Operators Confederation of India (BOCI)

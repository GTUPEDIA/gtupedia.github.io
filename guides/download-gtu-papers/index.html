How to download GTU question papers

GTUPEDIA organises official Gujarat Technological University exam papers by course, branch, semester, and subject code so you can reach the right PDF in a few clicks.

Step-by-step

  1. Open GTUPEDIA — visit the home page or use search if you know a subject code (e.g. BE03000081 or DI01000021).
  2. Select your course — choose BE, Diploma Engineering, BBA, BCA, MBA, MCA, B.Pharm, or another programme listed on the home page.
  3. Pick your branch — open your engineering or programme branch (e.g. Computer Engineering, branch 07).
  4. Find the semester — subjects are grouped by semester. Open the semester you are preparing for.
  5. Open the subject — click the subject name or code to see syllabus links and exam paper seasons.
  6. Download the PDF — choose Winter or Summer to open the official GTU question paper PDF on gtu.ac.in.

Search by subject code

If you already know the GTU subject code from your hall ticket or syllabus, type it in the search bar on the home page. GTUPEDIA will list matching subjects across branches and schemes where available.

NEW scheme codes often start with BE01, DI01, or BB01. Older codes are usually numeric (e.g. 3300001).

Common questions

Are these official GTU papers?

Yes. GTUPEDIA links to PDFs hosted on GTU’s official website. We catalogue which subjects and exam seasons are available so you know before you click.

Why is my subject missing?

Coverage grows each exam season. If a paper was recently released on GTU’s site, it may take a short time to appear here. You can also check gtu.ac.in directly.

Does GTUPEDIA cover Diploma Engineering?

Yes. Open DI — Diploma In Engineering on the home page for NEW, OLD, and Credit-to-Degree subjects across diploma branches.
